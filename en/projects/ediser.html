Technical Expert Unreal Engine 5

2023

I joined ENPC-Ediser, leader in driving simulators for driving school in France, to help the development team move from an aging homemade game engine to Unreal Engine 5. The project was to create a large open world called "Simuland" passing through a city and driving training circuits.

My mission was to create a graphic production pipeline that budgets for meshes polygons, textures resolution, optimization techniques such as baking, uses of simple collisions boxes etc...
My work mainly consisted of reading the doc, articles, watching conferences, and synthesizing all the information that could serve us in a docoument. I tested the different solutions and created guides to reproduce the results.
