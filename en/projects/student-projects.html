Student projects

On this page you'll see my games made while I was a student at Creajeux.

2022: Third year
Chrome Arena Infinite
On my last student project, I assumed the lead programmer role in a team of 4 programmers, 5 artists and 1 sound designer. We decided to make an arena shooter, in a sci-fi high-tech strongly inspired by Ratchet & Clank universe, especially the arena sections. This year, we must do the project on Unreal Engine 4.
I was responsible for the main gameplay, meaning controls, movements, combat and animations implementation of the player character. For this I wrote a GDD which concentrates the project's guidelines and our objectives. I created upgrade diagrams of the character and his weapons.
  • Movements
    Movements are fast and twitchy, the player controls a combat robot that can run, jump (and even double jump!), dash, use a jetpack and a grappling hook that hooks on any surface!

  • Combat
    The player has by default a cyber-flail and a plasma pistol. He will be able to buy new weapons in the shop with the currency, the chrome, that he recovers by eliminating the enemies. Each weapon has 2 fire mode, the secondary must be unlocked. There are stats upgrade (damage, ammunition, rate of fire...) but also in weapon behavior (seeking rocket, laser that spreads to nearby enemies).

  • Animations
    There has been a lot of work on the animations and their implementation, an aim offset for when looking in the air or on the ground, blends for multidirectional movements, other blends for movements + melee attacks, some attacks are root motion (it means, it's the animation that takes control of the character), IKs for large weapons held with 2 hands.
Chrome Arena Infinite is a project of which I am very proud of, it looks very good (thank the artists), and it is fun thanks to the atmosphere, the music, the feedback etc... We got the mark of 15/20 by a jury of video game professionals. We had had the idea to take over the project and release it on Steam, but in the end it we didn't make it. I still, ported the project on Unreal Engine 5 and replaced all the blueprint code with C++ and redone the logic of the bots with the BehaviorTree and the EQS.
I recommand to watch and to try this game, it's really fun!
2021: Second year
Bacchiatari
Bacchiatari is a third-person action combat game made by 4 programmers and 4 artists on Unity. My role gameplay programmer generalist.
  • I made the inventory system for the player so he can pickup weapons and select one from a weapon wheel.
  • I made a checkpoint system in case the player dies he starts again from this point.
  • I made a narative tutorial that tells the story of the elementary spirits seeking help from the hero while explaining what he has to do.
  • I also reworked the AI system so it's more modular and easy to use.
  • I also implemented some ambiant and feedback sounds like a waterfall, crackling fire, enemies that grunt when taking damage, the player jumping...
