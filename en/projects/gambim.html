Intern VR developper

August 2022 → December 2022

After Creajeux, I completed my internship with the company Gambi-M, which produces training and visit software for high security sites such as nuclear power plants.

Gambi-M performs laser 3D scans to create environments that are then integrated into Unity. It is therefore necessary to be able to transform these point clouds into polygonal meshes, but this makes extremely heavy objects that need to be refined. My first mission was to research tools to accelerate and facilitate this process. We stopped on PiXYZ (created by Dassaut, bought by Unity since), a software capable of mesh tesselation and decimation, which can even be scripted through Python code. Useful for automating the work of dozens of point clouds and meshes on workstations.

Then I was able to create a proof of concept in VR for a client on Unity in complete autonomy. It was my first experience to develop for VR. I used Git bash locally to practice and it saved the project the day after a small change of code, nothing worked anymore. Fortunately a small git revert and it was settled 😓.

Once this project was completed and validated, I was tasked with testing and comparing the different augmented reality solutions on Unity. So I conducted a test project between AR Fundation (the Unity solution) and Vuforia (a third-party solution) on Unity.
