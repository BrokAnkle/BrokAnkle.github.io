About

Who I am ?

I'am Arnaud GUYMONT, game developper, bachelor graduate at the Creajeux school in 2022. As many game dev, I myself am a gamer from as long as I can remember. I grew up with the Gameboy, Nintendo 64, Playstation... I've always wondered how to make a video game. I thought geniuses with glasses or magicians code every pixel of the screen with 0s and 1s. Although I don’t wear glasses or practice magic, today I’m proud to be one of them.

With more than 3 years of professional experience, mainly in serious games and virtual reality, I always aim to create unique and satisfying experiences for players. Whether this experience is for fun or educational purposes, my goal is to make it intuitive and memorable. I always look to expand my range of skills, if a challenge arises, it is with pleasure that I take it in hand. It is important for me not to stay in a comfort zone.

Gameplay, systems, UI, tools, documentation and even team lead. I can wear many hats.

Browse this website to know more about my skills, my work experiences and my ambitions.

Education

  • 2018 - 2022

    Creajeux: Bachelor in game programming

      In 2018 I joined Creajeux school at Nîmes. During my first year, we saw the fundamentals of video games and made our first games in Lua with the LÖVE2D, framework wich later will be used to make Balatro!

      My second year was dedicated to the C programming language. We were learning the language, pointers, and different types of data structures and algorithms that we then applied in our projects with the CSFML library.

      In the third year, we greatly expanded our surroundings with our first game engine: Unity. With that, we necessarily had to learn C#. We had other classes on C++ where we made our little game engine with the SFML by making a small Warcraft(rendering, sprite animation, pathfinding entities...). We has also seen some new features such as the programming tools for audio, and python for scripting Blender.

      Finally, the fourth and final year focused on Unreal Engine. The courses being mainly based on Blueprint, I have always tried C++ whenever I could. We had received some courses on network programming, but this year was mainly about preparing for entry into the professional sector. Much time spent working on resumes, cover letters, how and where to find an internship, etc...

  • 2015

    Certificate to serve in the French Navy Reserve as Rifleman

    After my PMM, I went to do 2 weeks of Initial Military Training of the Reservist in Lorient to join the Navy Riflemen Reserve. I was assigned to the Battalion Detroyat, which is located at the Toulon Arsenal. I ensured the armed protection-defense of military sites there such as naval bases, naval air forces, ammunition dumps, pyrotechnics, and even the aircraft carrier Charles de Gaulle. My transition to the army has greatly shaped my character and my approach to teamwork. Cohesion is the key. I left the Navy in 2024, it was too complicated to reconcile professional life with the reserve. I was at the rank of Sergeant.

  • 2015

    PMM

    When Navy officers visited my high school, I agreed to join the PMM because I have always been interested in the army. This first step in the Navy allowed me to see the universe from the inside. It happened every other Saturday and we were taught what the Navy and the army in general were, the different professions and their daily lives. We went for 1 week to the Toulon Arsenal to meet military and do activities, such as rescue operations and shooting with Famas. We were even offered to take the boat license (free of charge), which I did and succeeded.

Ambitions

Not even graduated yet, I already had the idea to create my own development studio. But is it realistic for a young man fresh out of school to embark on a sector as competitive as video games without experience? I thought it is more prudent to gain experience, meet and connect with other developers. Being able to see from the inside how projects are built and organized in different companies would be the best way for me to learn and obviously, at the same time, make interesting projects.

In the short to medium term (~5 ans), my ambition is to keep gaining experience, stregthen my expertise, and climb the hierarchical ladder. I have already held a lead role, first in school, which went very well, then one in the professional environment where I encountered more difficulties but I prefer to see the glass half full: it allowed me to learn a lot about team management and I came out strengthened and I would like to repeat the experience.

In the long term, I see myself in a responsible role, wheter it's senior lead programmer, technical directror, producer, project manager, or even with my own studio.

I think I'm a person who takes a step back and likes to have a global vision of the projects he works on. I seek to be inspiring with my peers and to motivate by example, positive encouragement, and highlighting team members.
